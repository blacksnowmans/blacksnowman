South Korea’s Ruling Party Collides with Itself: Leaked Tapes, Sect Allegations, and a High-Stakes Legal Battle

November 21, 2026

On November 20, inside the main chamber of South Korea’s National Assembly in Seoul, two top figures of the ruling Democratic Party sat side-by-side in frosty silence. Representative Kim Min-seok, the head of the party, and Supreme Council Member Choi Min-hee stared straight ahead, carefully avoiding eye contact while news cameras flashed around them. Physical proximity could not mask the widening gulf between them—or the profound crisis threatening to tear their party apart from within.

The Democratic Party of Korea, which currently holds both the presidency under President Lee Jae-myung and a controlling majority in parliament following the impeachment and imprisonment of former official Mr. Yoon Suk-yeol, is wrestling with a sudden double-barreled scandal. On one front, a leaked audio transcript involving Supreme Council Member Choi has triggered an intense factional feud and a formal disciplinary hearing set for November 27. On the other, Representative Kim faces explosive allegations regarding past ties to Shincheonji, a controversial religious sect, culminating in more than 1,000 party members filing a court injunction to suspend his leadership duties.

What began as isolated political controversies has rapidly morphed into an existential struggle for control over South Korea’s ruling party, exposing deep fault lines between party factions, media influencers, and the rank-and-file membership.

The Choi Min-hee Transcript Scandal and the Impending Ethics Hearing

The immediate catalyst for the internal fallout was the public release of an audio recording featuring Supreme Council Member Choi Min-hee. As the contents of the tape circulated through domestic media, existing factional tensions within the Democratic Party exploded into public view. A coalition of 55 freshman lawmakers—first-term members of the National Assembly—issued a joint statement demanding Choi’s immediate resignation from the Supreme Council.

However, Choi’s allies inside the party, particularly the "pro-Chung" faction (친청계) and her core supporters, strongly resisted the pressure. They condemned the resignation demands as a coordinated hit job by rival factions seeking to alter the party's balance of power. Online message boards and social media channels instantly transformed into digital battlegrounds, with grassroots members locked in fierce debates over whether Choi should step down.

Recognizing the severity of the rift, the Democratic Party’s Ethics Committee formally decided to convene on November 27 to deliberate on potential disciplinary measures against Choi. The announcement of formal ethics proceedings drew immediate commentary across the political spectrum:

  • Lawmaker Jung Sung-ho stated in an interview that formal disciplinary action against Choi had become unavoidable given the scale of the controversy. However, Jung publicly criticized senior lawmaker Choo Mi-ae for jumping into the fray, calling her intervention inappropriate.
  • Political commentator and Professor Chin Jung-kwon offered a starker assessment, observing that the Democratic Party was already in a state of "psychological split." Chin warned that if the Ethics Committee delivers a harsh punishment against Choi, it will spark an all-out internal war.
  • Lawmaker Kwak Sang-eon, the son-in-law of the late former President Roh Moo-hyun, accused party leadership of blatant double standards. Kwak noted that when Choi faced intense public scrutiny over a wedding gift money controversy in the past, leadership moved aggressively to protect her. He argued it was hypocritical for those same figures to now demand her immediate resignation and party exit over a audio recording.

The controversy took another turn when prominent political commentator and broadcaster Kim Ou-joon used his show to defend Choi, arguing that the public backlash and proposed disciplinary actions against her were wildly disproportionate. Following Kim’s broadcast, senior party figures including Representative Jung Cheong-rae and Representative Choo Mi-ae mobilized to shield Choi from further attack.

That sequence of events provoked a sharp backlash from non-mainstream members and the 55 freshman lawmakers. Critics voiced anger that top party figures appeared to alter their political maneuvers based on the commentary of an outside media figure, arguing that the party’s formal ethics mechanisms were being undermined by external influencers.

Sect Allegations and the Legal Battle to Suspend Representative Kim Min-seok

While Choi Min-hee fights for her political survival on the Supreme Council, party leader Kim Min-seok is facing a formidable legal and ethical challenge of his own. The controversy centers on persistent allegations regarding Kim’s past connections and alleged secret alliance with entities affiliated with Shincheonji—a secretive religious group that has long drawn intense public skepticism and political scrutiny in South Korea.

Dissatisfaction over Kim’s handling of these allegations reached a breaking point on November 20. Lee Jong-won, head of the political media platform Sisa Tapa TV, joined 1,101 registered Democratic Party members in filing a formal injunction at the Seoul District Court seeking the immediate suspension of Representative Kim’s duties as party leader.

In their petition, the members asserted that Kim had failed to provide transparent explanations regarding his past interactions with Shincheonji-linked organizations, thereby inflicting severe damage on the party’s moral authority. The spectacle of more than a thousand active members taking their own party leader to court represents a severe blow to Kim's authority.

Pressure is also building from within the legislative caucus. Representative Lee Sung-yoon called on the party’s Ethics Committee to officially open a parallel investigation into Representative Kim. Lee argued that proceeding with a disciplinary hearing against Choi Min-hee while ignoring the serious allegations surrounding the party leader would violate basic principles of fairness and organizational consistency.

Recent public opinion polls published on November 20 underscore how deeply these controversies have unsettled both the general public and the party’s base:

  • National Demand for Transparency: According to a poll commissioned by Kuki News, 80.5% of South Korean respondents stated that the Democratic Party must publicly disclose the complete, unredacted results of its internal investigation into Shincheonji ties.
  • Divided Party Base: A separate survey by Media Tomato revealed significant friction among self-identified Democratic Party supporters. While 46.4% felt the issue should be wrapped up under current leadership, 20.4% demanded Representative Kim’s outright resignation, and another 19.6% insisted on a formal public apology. Combined, 40% of the party’s own core supporters are demanding concrete accountability.

Public Relations Battles Versus the Silence on the Assembly Floor

Faced with escalating internal conflict and legal pressure, Representative Kim Min-seok sought to signal business-as-usual through a public-facing policy event on the morning of November 20. Kim visited a public daycare center in Seoul to tour its kitchen facilities, assist in serving lunches to young children, and host a roundtable discussion with childcare workers on expanding government subsidies for infant meal programs.

The policy event was designed to steer national headlines back toward the ruling party's economic and welfare agenda. However, as news broke throughout the morning regarding the court injunction filed by 1,101 party members, the public relations effort was largely eclipsed. Kim declined to answer questions from reporters regarding the internal crisis during his morning rounds.

By the afternoon, the tension shifted directly to the floor of the National Assembly. During a closed-door general caucus meeting and subsequent plenary session, Kim Min-seok and Choi Min-hee sat next to each other on the front benches. Cameras repeatedly captured the pair sitting in icy silence, each staring in opposite directions.

Behind the scenes, frantic political maneuvering took place along the corridors of the Assembly building:

  • Representative Kim was observed holding low-whisper consultations with Floor Leader Han Byung-do, as well as senior lawmakers Shin Jeong-hoon and Park Sun-won, to gauge caucus sentiment and coordinate legislative scheduling.
  • Concurrently, Choi Min-hee conferred with trusted allies, including Representative Kwon Chil-seung, mapping out legal and political strategies ahead of her November 27 ethics hearing.

What to Watch Next as the November 27 Deadline Approaches

The Democratic Party stands at a critical juncture. The convergence of an impending ethics ruling, a court battle over leadership authority, and public demands for transparency over religious sect ties has created an unstable political environment for the ruling majority.

As the party moves toward the end of the month, several key indicators will determine whether this conflict can be contained or if it will trigger a broader political realignment:

  • The November 27 Ethics Committee Ruling: The severity of the decision regarding Choi Min-hee will be decisive. A harsh penalty, such as suspension or expulsion, risks provoking an open revolt from the pro-Chung faction and allied media figures. Conversely, a light reprimand will likely incite the 55 freshman lawmakers and non-mainstream factions to openly challenge the leadership's moral authority.
  • The Seoul District Court's Injunction Decision: If the court chooses to hear or grant the petition brought by the 1,101 party members against Representative Kim Min-seok, the Democratic Party will be plunged into an unprecedented leadership vacuum. Even a decision to hear oral arguments will prolong the legal uncertainty surrounding the party chair.
  • The Push for Disclosure on Sect Ties: With over 80% of the public demanding full disclosure of internal findings regarding Shincheonji, how Representative Kim and the Democratic Party leadership choose to handle these investigative reports will test the party's public standing and moral credibility ahead of upcoming legislative battles.
